End-to-end flow: request to cash

The quote journey as Steven described it. Orange marks a check that lives in someone's head. Red marks a manual or error-prone step. Map these onto the 16 workflow steps in the brief.

Two routes. Standard quotes (driveways, sheds, usually under about 1,000 m³) are handled by the costing team alone. Bigger quotes (dairy floors, subdivisions, power stations) go to the Technical team for specifications, plus cement and aggregate quotes. No formal trigger separates them; technical complexity can override volume.
